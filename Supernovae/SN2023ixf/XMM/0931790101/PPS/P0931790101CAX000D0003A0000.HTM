Mission_Name: CHANDRA


Description:

The Chandra Source Catalog (CSC) is ultimately intended to be the definitive catalog of X-ray sources 
detected by the Chandra X-ray Observatory. To achieve that goal, the catalog will be released to the 
user community in a series of increments with increasing capability. The second major release of the 
CSC (release 2.0) includes data for roughly 315,000 X-ray sources on the sky extracted from 10,382 
Chandra ACIS and HRC-I imaging observations released publicly through the end of 2014. 

Users are kindly requested to acknowledge their use of the Chandra Source Catalog in any resulting 
publications. http://cxc.harvard.edu/csc2/cite.html

The version operating at Strasbourg Observatory/CDS uses the results of an ADQL query
http://cxc.harvard.edu/csc2/cli/index.html and only lists a subset of all columns available in the 
database view.
