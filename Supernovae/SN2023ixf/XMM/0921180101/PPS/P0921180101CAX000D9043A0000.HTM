Abstract:
    We present the 1SXPS (Swift-XRT Point Source) catalogue of 151,524
    X-ray point-sources detected by the Swift-XRT in 8 years of operation.
    The catalogue covers 1905 square degrees distributed approximately
    uniformly on the sky. We analyze the data in two ways. First we
    consider all observations individually, for which we have a typical
    sensitivity of ~3e-13 erg.cm-2s^-1` (0.3-10 keV). Then we co-add
    all data covering the same location on the sky: these images have a
    typical sensitivity of ~9e-14 erg cm-2s-1 (0.3-10 keV). Our sky
    coverage is nearly 2.5 times that of 3XMM-DR4, although the catalog
    is a factor of ~1.5 less sensitive. The median position error is 5.5"
    (90% confidence), including systematics. Our source detection method
    improves on that used in previous XRT catalogues and we report >68,000
    new X-ray sources. The goals and observing strategy of the Swift
    satellite allow us to probe source variability on multiple timescales,
    and we find ~30,000 variable objects in our catalog. For every source
    we give positions, fluxes, time series (in four energy bands and two
    hardness ratios), estimates of the spectral properties, spectra and
    spectral fits for the brightest sources, and variability probabilities
    in multiple energy bands and timescales.

Description:
    The 1SXPS catalogue is a point-source catalogue built from all
    Swift-XRT Photon Counting mode data of at least 100-s in duration
    gathered prior to 2012 October 12 (Swift was launched in 2004
    November). It contains positions, fluxes, spectral details,
    variability information and a flag indicating the reliability of the
    detection for 151,524 objects. Various values (e.g. the count rates)
    are given in 4 bands:
      Total   = 0.3-10 keV
      Soft    = 0.3-1 keV
      Medium  = 1-2 keV
      Hard    = 2-10 keV
    The source detection was performed in each of these bands independently,
    and the results combined to give this final catalogue.
